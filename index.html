Help & support

Your pocket film darkroom

Create with SpektraGo on iPhone, Duo, and iPad, from your first import to the final export.

Get started

Import images from the library using the system photo picker or Files. Tap a photo to open the editor, choose film and paper, then adjust exposure, grain, and color. SpektraGo keeps a separate copy and does not overwrite your original at the import source.

Compare and export

Press and hold the preview to see the original, or enable the draggable split view in the comparison menu. Use the top export button to choose JPG, HEIC, PNG, or 16-bit TIFF, then save to Photos or use the system share sheet. Select photos in the library to export a batch.

Duo and iPad

Compact screens use bottom controls; wider screens show sidebars as space allows. Your current photo and editing settings remain in place when you rotate the device or fold and unfold Duo. Collapse the sidebars for a larger preview.

Storage and recovery

Open About → Storage to view originals, thumbnails, and temporary files. Temporary files can be cleared separately. Recently removed photos can be restored for seven days. These actions affect only copies stored by the app.

RAW files and large exports

RAW and camera-model compatibility depend on the device’s system decoder. If a full-size export fails, try a 4096 px or 2048 px long edge and check available device storage. When contacting support, include the file format, device model, and operating system version.

Free features and optional tips

All editing and export features are free. The App Store edition offers optional one-time tips, with no subscriptions or extra features. Apple processes payments. Pending or unavailable purchases do not prevent you from using the app.
