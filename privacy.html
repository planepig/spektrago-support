SpektraGo

Privacy policy

Updated: November 5, 2026

SpektraGo is a film darkroom app that processes photos on your device. It requires no app account, contains no ads, and integrates no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Photos and editing data

You select images to import through the system photo picker or Files. The app stores copies, thumbnails, photo names, and editing settings in its own storage, then decodes and renders photos on your device. This content is not sent to a developer-operated server. Importing and editing do not overwrite the original at the import source.

Photo permission and sharing

The app requests permission to add photos when you save exports to your photo library. When you choose to share, the exported files are handed to the app or service you select, whose privacy policy applies.

Local storage and deletion

Removed photos can remain in Recently Removed for seven days so that you can restore them. The app cleans up expired copies. Cleanup affects only copies stored by the app. You can clear temporary files from storage management in About. Uninstalling removes local app data; retention and restoration of device or cloud backups depend on your system settings and Apple's applicable policies.

Optional tips

All editing and export features are free. Optional one-time tips are processed by the Apple App Store and unlock no additional features. The app uses StoreKit to verify transactions and handles transaction status on your device. The developer does not receive your payment card or payment account details. Apple's handling of purchase and payment information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Tracking and collection

The app does not use advertising identifiers, track you across apps, or upload photos, usage logs, or device identifiers to the developer. Export preferences, file-size access, and rendering timers are used only to provide app functionality.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact planepig@outlook.com. If you choose to email the developer, the developer receives your email address and the content you provide, and uses them only to respond to and handle your request.

Support website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub may process standard web-request information under its privacy statement.
